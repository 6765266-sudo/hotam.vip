“Who Am I?” — The Day Rabbi Yoel Teitelbaum Was Installed in Satmar

Thousands filled the streets of Satmar. A military band led the procession to the Great Synagogue. When Rabbi Yoel Teitelbaum reached the bimah, his first words were: “Who am I?”

After six years of disagreement over the rabbinate, the two camps had reached an agreement. The newspaper’s correspondent follows the day from the rabbi’s quiet arrival with his family to the crowded synagogue, his address on communal unity and the celebration that lasted into the night.
