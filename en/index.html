02 / FROM QUESTION TO RECORD

You bring the question.
We map the next step.

You can write in your preferred language. We’ll use your starting information to assess the research scope before agreeing on work, cost or delivery.

  1. 01
    Tell us what you know

    Names, places, approximate dates and what you would like to discover. Even a small starting point can help.

  2. 02
    Agree on a focused search

    We check source availability and discuss a realistic scope and a quote before paid work begins.

  3. 03
    Follow the evidence

    Findings are linked to their sources. A possible lead is kept distinct from an established connection.

  4. 04
    Receive something you can keep

    An agreed report or organized set of findings, with references and clear notes on what remains unknown.

Before we begin.

Can I write in my own language?

Yes. Write your enquiry in the language that is easiest for you and tell us your preferred reply language. We will confirm what language support your project needs before work begins.

Can you research any country or community?

Enquiries are welcome from all backgrounds. Feasibility depends on the place, period, access rules and surviving records. We assess each request individually.

Will you definitely find my ancestors?

Research can uncover new evidence, but a particular record or family connection cannot be guaranteed. We will explain the limits and distinguish findings from leads.

Will my family story be published?

Submitting an enquiry does not authorize publication. Any proposed public use of your family material will be discussed separately.
