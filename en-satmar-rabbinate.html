When Rabbi Yoel Teitelbaum Was Proposed as Rabbi of Satmar

In 1928, the Satmar rabbinate fell vacant. The nomination of Rabbi Yoel Teitelbaum brought longstanding differences within the Orthodox community into the open.

A contemporary newspaper follows the negotiations and explains what each side wanted from the next rabbi. The story offers the background to the installation that followed six years later.
